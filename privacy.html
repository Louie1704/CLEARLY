Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20th, 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account details you give us, such as your name and email address.
  • Payment details, which are handled by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Usage data, such as the pages you visit and the device and browser you use.
  • Messages you send us, for example when you contact support.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to create and manage your account, process payments, provide support, improve the site, and send important notices about the service. We only send marketing emails if you have agreed to receive them.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run Clearly, such as payment processors, email services, and analytics tools, and only as needed for them to do their work. We may also share information if the law requires it.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar tools to keep you signed in and to understand how the site is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as expected.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for a reasonable time afterward, for example to meet legal or accounting requirements. When we no longer need it, we delete it or make it anonymous.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, export it, or delete it. You can also unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time. To make a request, email us at the address below.

7. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit and at rest, and we limit who can access it. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make important changes, we will update the date at the top and tell you by email or on the site.

9. Children

Clearly is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.
