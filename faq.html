Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about plans, billing, and support. Can't find what you need? Contact us below.

Is there a free plan?

Yes,there is. The Free plan includes one project and basic analytics, and it doesn't expire. You can upgrade whenever you need more.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts. Cancel from your account settings and you keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can move up or down between Free, Pro, and Team at any time. When you upgrade, the change applies right away, and when you downgrade, it starts at your next billing date.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major debit and credit cards. Your card details are handled by our payment provider and are never stored on our servers.

Do you offer discounts for teams or yearly billing?

Yearly billing gives you two months free compared with paying monthly. For teams of ten or more, contact us and we'll work out a custom price.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we make daily backups. You can export or delete your data at any time.

How do I get help?

Send us an email and a real person will reply within one business day. Pro and Team plans get priority replies.

Still have questions?

Our team replies within one business day.
